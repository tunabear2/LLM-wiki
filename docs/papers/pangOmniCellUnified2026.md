## Abstract

OmniCell is a tissue-contextual transcriptomic foundation model pretrained on 67 million dissociated and spatially resolved profiles. It integrates gene identity, expression magnitude, and tissue context to link transcriptional programs with cellular neighborhoods and anatomical context, supporting spatial cell identity, anatomical domain, composition reconstruction, contextual gene similarity, and virtual perturbation analyses.

## 2. 핵심 아이디어

%% begin core-idea %%
Cell state는 expression만이 아니라 그 cell이 놓인 anatomical niche와 neighborhood에 의해 의미가 달라진다. OmniCell은 dissociated single-cell profile과 spatial profile을 함께 pretraining해 cell-type program, tissue-aligned gene module, spatial domain, contextual gene relationship을 같은 representation에서 다룬다.
%% end core-idea %%

## 3. 내 연구에 적용할 아이디어

%% begin research-ideas %%
Kidney transplant biopsy에서는 rejection immune infiltrate가 tubulitis, glomerulitis, peritubular capillaritis 같은 tissue context와 함께 해석되어야 한다. OmniCell식 context-aware embedding은 scRNA-seq immune state를 spatial lesion neighborhood와 연결하고, rejection margin 또는 injury niche별 gene function 차이를 비교하는 데 직접적이다.
%% end research-ideas %%
